# Siamese Embedded — six distinct Business Model Canvases and experiment links

**v1 · 2026-11-20 · unvalidated architecture hypotheses**  
**Prerequisite:** [Activity-System Ex-Ante Exploration](ACTIVITY_SYSTEM_EX_ANTE_EXPLORATION_V0.md). No Canvas is endorsed as a viable market until its own actor, economic, legal and technical evidence passes. Do not pool O1/O5 customer demand or use O6 as a civilian event product.

## Canvas selection protocol

1. Define neutral customer job and incumbent baseline (O0); identify buyer/operator/authorizer separately.
2. Instantiate and challenge the `Content → Structure → Governance` chain, then the NICE mechanism and weakest dependency.
3. Capture nine Canvas blocks, with **hypothesis rather than evidence** status.
4. Run three option-specific falsifying experiments; link to [physical experiment coverage matrix](PHYSICAL_PROTOTYPE_HYPOTHESIS_EVIDENCE_V1.md).
5. Only then allocate engineering BRs and budget under a specific selected architecture; preserve STOP/PIVOT/PERSEVERE.

## O1 — Non-identifying event signal device (sale/rental)

| Canvas block | Proposed hypothesis — not validated |
|---|---|
| **Customer Segments** | Event organizers, recurring venues and security integrators; distinct payer vs shift operator |
| **Value Propositions** | Secure non-identifying entrance-condition alerts, low nuisance burden, rapid fixed-node deployment; never a civilian 1:N face blacklist |
| **Channels** | B2B technical integrator, permitted venue demonstration, event security partner referrals |
| **Customer Relationships** | Site survey, installation, operator training, maintenance and incident review |
| **Revenue Streams** | Per-event rental + setup fee, device sale or annual support; prices and demand unknown |
| **Key Resources** | COTS fixed sensor, energy/radio/firmware stack, approved mount, test evidence, equipment inventory |
| **Key Activities** | Discover incidents, calibrate compliant sensors, deploy, dispatch, troubleshoot, inspect, redeploy |
| **Key Partners** | Venue, safety operator, licensed integrator, RF gateway vendor, compliance/DPO assessor |
| **Cost Structure** | Device asset depreciation, travel/install/remove, insurance, monitoring/false alarm handling, support and lifecycle |

**Activity-system governance:** Integrator owns equipment; organizer pays and controls venue; operators review alerts; legal/venue consent controls access.

**Strong rival architecture:** Existing guards with radios, manual counters and compliant low-cost presence sensors.

**Primary causal vulnerability:** Operator might face more alerts, not less.

**Experiments:** `O1-D1`, `O1-D2`, `O1-D3`. All have their own customer/engineering evidence and pre-registered decision gates in the full [experiment matrix](PHYSICAL_PROTOTYPE_HYPOTHESIS_EVIDENCE_V1.md); see also the 2026-11-20 business dossier v3.

## O3 — Voluntary verification 1:1 integration

| Canvas block | Proposed hypothesis — not validated |
|---|---|
| **Customer Segments** | Venue access platforms that can demonstrate a lawful, genuinely optional verification need |
| **Value Propositions** | Local 1:1 verification when a visitor presents a credential; accessible non-biometric alternative |
| **Channels** | Ticket/access OEMs and systems integrators with verified legal scope |
| **Customer Relationships** | Integration, data-protection audit support, accessibility and acceptance maintenance |
| **Revenue Streams** | Integration license, bounded engineering fee, support for lawful deployments |
| **Key Resources** | Validated 1:1 verifier, credential bridge, consent/fallback journey, secure provisioning |
| **Key Activities** | Data-protection gate, verify eligibility, integrate access controls, user studies, QA and updates |
| **Key Partners** | Credential issuer, venue controller, DPO/legal assessor, accessibility and fallback operator |
| **Cost Structure** | Algorithmic/biometric quality study, fallback staffing, integration, audit and support |

**Activity-system governance:** Controller/legal authority sets purpose; users need meaningful choice; vendor cannot create legal basis.

**Strong rival architecture:** Ticket/QR/badge scan plus staff assistance.

**Primary causal vulnerability:** Biometric friction may worsen queues or undermine meaningful voluntary choice.

**Experiments:** `O3-V1`, `O3-V2`, `O3-V3`. All have their own customer/engineering evidence and pre-registered decision gates in the full [experiment matrix](PHYSICAL_PROTOTYPE_HYPOTHESIS_EVIDENCE_V1.md); see also the 2026-11-20 business dossier v3.

| **Value Propositions** | Calibrated, reproducible accuracy–energy–latency–memory comparisons that inform an engineering decision |

**Primary causal vulnerability:** Reproducibility may be technically achieved but buyers may not pay.

## O6 — Restricted authorized institutional research platform

| Canvas block | Proposed hypothesis — not validated |
|---|---|
| **Customer Segments** | Only institution with documented narrowly authorized statutory mission and accountable sponsor |
| **Value Propositions** | Controlled research on local 1:N technology with transparent uncertainty, review and audit |
| **Channels** | Formal R&D agreement, qualified tender/market consultation, authorized systems integrator |
| **Customer Relationships** | Strict scope, independent evaluation, change approval, audit and long-term maintenance |
| **Revenue Streams** | Bounded research contract; deployment/procurement only under separate lawful authorization |
| **Key Resources** | Qualified platform, trusted dataset custody, security assurance, evidence and legal authority |
| **Key Activities** | Legality approval, design-of-experiments, independent verification, human decision workflow, decommissioning |

**Activity-system governance:** Eligible authority owns narrowly lawful purpose; scientific/oversight reviewers have hard veto rights.

**Strong rival architecture:** Existing legal investigation and credential-checking procedures without generalized 1:N.

**Primary causal vulnerability:** Police/defence status does not automatically authorize facial remote identification.

## Governance of downstream technical artifacts

The same PCB, metrology probe or local matching function can be an O1 cost, an O4 licensable feature, an O5 test subject or an O6 research input. That **does not** mean it validates all six Canvas value propositions. Trace it as `AS_ID → OPTION → HYPOTHESIS → TEST → evidence → decision → BR → CAP → FEATURE → UC → acceptance`.

No paid pilots, user interviews, willingness-to-pay tests or sponsor approvals are represented as completed. All thresholds are *draft decision gates*, to freeze before their actual experiment.

Source methodology: ESSEC Croque Gourmet pre-case activity-system analysis; Zott & Amit 2010, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lrp.2009.07.004 .
