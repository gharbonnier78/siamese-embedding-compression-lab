## Decision summary

**GO** for cheap, evidence-gated discovery and an independent simulated/consented engineering research track. **NO GO** for a general-purpose 1:N face-watchlist identification product deployed by civilian event organisers. **NOT YET** for custom PCB manufacture, technical/biometric superiority claims or commercial launch.

**Fixed event installation:** the EO/RGB + NIR device is wall/support-mounted, not wearable. It operates with PoE/DC and protected backup; 1,000 encounters/hour over 10 h/day for five days is a stress hypothesis, not field evidence. The experiment PC is separate from autonomous matching and from the organiser's security-radio alert receiver.

## Regulatory no-go and precision

“Blacklist AI” is NOT a precise stand-alone legal prohibition. In the EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(h), real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces **for law enforcement purposes** is generally prohibited save narrowly defined exceptions with national enabling law, strict necessity/proportionality and generally judicial/independent administrative authorisation. Public authority employment alone is not permission. Military, defence and national security **exclusive purposes** may be outside the AI Act's scope under Art. 2, not a general law-enforcement exemption. Under GDPR Art. 9, biometric processing for unique identification requires special legal conditions; a civilian event cannot simply claim a “security blacklist” and avoid them. France's event algorithmic-video framework, Article 10 of Law 2023-380 as amended in 2026, expressly excludes facial recognition and biometric identification in that experiment. The exact site/purpose must be legally reviewed before any pilot.

## Lean entrepreneurship experiment sequence

1. **Problem before product:** conduct >=15 interviews across >=5 organisations; document actual event incidents, false interventions, install cost and purchasing decisions. At least 8/15 should describe independently recurring material pain; otherwise revise the segment.
2. **Actor and veto map:** budget buyer, shift operator, venue authoriser, DPO/legal, affected attendees, integrator, independent test/certification authority. Treat missing permission as stop, not a low likelihood.
3. **Architectural diversity before ranking:** compare non-identifying sensor alerts, managed service, voluntary 1:1, OEM licensing, paid benchmark R&D and narrow legally approved sovereign R&D. Apply Zott & Amit content / structure / governance and NICE; judge against simpler alternatives.
4. **Minimum discriminating evidence:** >=2 written design partner commitments, ideally >=1 paid discovery/pilot; measure actual operator workflow, avoid synthetic personas as purchase proof.
5. **Benchmark with adversarial baselines:** fixed site, day/night, rain/cold/heat, 1,000 encounters/hour bursts, independently metered J/accepted event, p95/p99, RF delivery and human acknowledgement.
6. **Economics:** price only after quotes; total event contribution must include setup/removal labour, power, RF gateway, insurance, compliance and false alert handling.
7. **Decision:** dated STOP/PIVOT/PERSEVERE independent reviewer record. Never compensate for a legal NO GO by attractive measured speed.

## Numeric targets — ALL are proposed, not measured

- Installation: <=30 min per node by trained two-person crew across >=3 trial sites, all safety checks passed.
- Security alert: >=99% console receipt within 10 s in *defined* RF site test; network receipt distinct from human acknowledgement; lost events never silent.
- Autonomy: no external PC/inference service needed; local functions survive a scripted 8 h network disconnect with zero unexplained data loss.
- Non-identifying detection: >=95% valid detection in frozen nominal scenario and <=1 false operator dispatch/node/10h; compare against human/sensor-only baseline.
- 1m synthetic gallery: zero OOM, ANN retrieval recall >=99.5% against exact nearest neighbour at declared p95 search <=150 ms; NOT a biometric claim.
- Compression research: require quality gate, >=20% *search* J/query and >=10% *total* J/accepted event benefit vs matched 512D reference; otherwise STOP.
- Engineering evidence: independent reference power meter, calibration IDs, software/model/gallery hashes, per-mode thermal/voltage probes, all critical failures explicitly reported.
- Business: >=2 written committed design partners, >=1 funded pilot or equivalent approved opportunity, positive variable contribution on >=2 genuine priced event trials.

A single false positive in a legally consequential identity flow can be unacceptable: these operational thresholds do NOT authorise a facial 1:N product. Biometric FPIR/FNIR, statistical power and intended-use standards must be separately pre-registered and reviewed.
