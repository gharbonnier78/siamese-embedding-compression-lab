## High-value questions for on-site conversations

1. Which enclosure polymers match operating/surface temperature, dimensional stability and repeated opening?
2. Which additive processes and post-processing are practical for low-volume iteration (fit coupons -> v1 -> v2)?
3. What NIR-transmissive window materials are suitable for 850/940 nm illumination (actual spectrum TBD) and glare management?
4. Which thermal interface, heat spreader, flame-retardancy and battery-pack isolation materials are appropriate for a compact fanless SBC?
5. What aging, environmental, UV, cleaning-fluid and repair/recycling tests would Arkema propose?
6. Could the Club introduce materials engineers or prototyping partners, not only showroom guides?
7. What can be shared under NDA/IP terms? Start with a non-sensitive problem statement and synthetic data only.
8. Who could help validate material choices and tolerances for a small first-run enclosure?
