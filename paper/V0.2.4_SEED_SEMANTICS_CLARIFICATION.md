# v0.2.4 reproducibility clarification — seed semantics

Date: 2026-08-23

## Origin

An independent reader reviewing the v0.2.3 manuscript identified an ambiguity between the PCA description and the later all-seeds decision rule. The manuscript said that PCA used scikit-learn randomized SVD with "a fixed seed", while a later section referred to the "stochastic 128D routes" and Tables 3/4 reported PCA over five seeds.

The reviewer correctly observed that the paper did not make the route-specific seed semantics explicit enough for a reader to determine whether PCA itself varied across the five seeds or whether only bootstrap Monte Carlo did.

## Authoritative implementation check

The implementation resolves the ambiguity:

- `PCAProjection` receives a `seed` and constructs `sklearn.decomposition.PCA(n_components=output_dim, svd_solver="randomized", random_state=seed)`.
- The experiment configuration freezes `training.seeds: [11, 29, 47, 71, 101]`.
- The TRAIN/VALIDATION split is frozen separately by `split_seed: 20260806`.

Therefore PCA is deterministic conditional on one seed, but it is refitted under each of the five preregistered model/projection seeds and can produce a different 128D basis for each seeded randomized-SVD realization. The five PCA rows/ranges are therefore legitimate and are not generated merely by changing the bootstrap Monte-Carlo stream.

## Corrected reader-facing wording

### PCA section

> In this repository PCA is fitted only on TRAIN endpoints, with 128 components, using scikit-learn's randomized SVD solver. The TRAIN/VALIDATION split is fixed once by the independent split seed `20260806`, while PCA is refitted under each of the five preregistered model/projection seeds `{11,29,47,71,101}` through scikit-learn's `random_state`. PCA is therefore deterministic conditional on a given seed, but its fitted 128D projection may differ across the five seeded realizations. The projected outputs are then L2-normalized before distances are evaluated.

### Seed section

> The three 128D candidate routes are evaluated under the same five preregistered model/projection seeds `{11,29,47,71,101}`. The role of the seed differs by route: it generates the Gaussian matrix for random projection, controls the randomized SVD solver for PCA, and controls initialization and training order for the Siamese projection. The data split itself is fixed independently by `split_seed = 20260806`. Raw 512D has no projection randomness and is therefore represented once.

The integrated v0.2.4 candidate also makes explicit that model/projection seeds and bootstrap Monte-Carlo random streams are conceptually different: the former change the generated/fitted 128D route, while the latter resample already frozen historical scores to estimate uncertainty.
